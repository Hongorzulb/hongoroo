Travel tips

China at a glance

Area: ~9.6 million km² • Population: 1.4+ billion • Languages: Mandarin (standard), many regional languages

Best seasons: Spring (Apr–May) & Autumn (Sep–Oct) for mild weather and clear skies.

福

Must-see & Iconic Places

Beijing — Forbidden City & the Great Wall

Ancient imperial palaces, Hutongs (historic lanes), and easy access to the Badaling & Mutianyu sections of the Great Wall.

Shanghai — Bund & modern skyline

Futuristic skyline, colonial Bund, Yuyuan Garden, and vibrant food & nightlife.

Xi'an — Terracotta Army

Home of the Terracotta Warriors; explore ancient city walls and Muslim Quarter snacks.

Guilin & Yangshuo — Karst landscapes

Iconic limestone peaks, scenic Li River cruises, cycling through rice terraces and riverside villages.

Chengdu — Giant pandas & Sichuan cuisine

Visit panda reserves and enjoy world-famous spicy Sichuan dishes (and tea culture).

Hong Kong — Harbor & urban culture

Harbor views, street food, markets, and easy day trips to outlying islands.

食

Food & Must-try Dishes

  • Peking Duck — Beijing specialty (crispy skin, pancakes, hoisin)
  • Dumplings (jiaozi / xiaolongbao) — North & East (try xiaolongbao in Shanghai)
  • Sichuan Hot Pot & Mapo Tofu — Spicy, numbing flavors from Sichuan peppercorns
  • Cantonese dim sum — Small plates & tea; popular in Guangdong & Hong Kong
  • Street snacks — Jianbing (savory pancake), skewers, candied fruit, and regional specialties
礼

Culture & Etiquette (short guide)

A few practical pointers to travel respectfully and smoothly:

  • Greetings: A handshake is common in urban areas; keep politeness and modesty.
  • Personal space: Cities are busy — be patient in queues and public transport.
  • Photography: Ask before photographing people (especially in rural areas).
  • Cash & apps: Cash is still useful; mobile payments (WeChat/Alipay) are ubiquitous — prepare alternatives if you can't use them.
  • Language: Learn a few Mandarin phrases; maps/screenshots help — many signs in big cities are bilingual.

Travel tips

¥
Currency: The Chinese currency is the yuan (CNY). Small shops may prefer cash.
📶
Connectivity: Consider a local SIM or portable Wi-Fi. Many foreign websites/apps may be limited — plan alternatives.
🕒
Timing: Peak travel during Golden Week (early October) and Chinese New Year — book in advance.
🧾
Receipts & documentation: Keep copies of bookings and IDs; many hotels require ID at check-in.
